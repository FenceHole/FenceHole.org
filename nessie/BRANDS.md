### How to use credentials

Pull **one or two relevant ones per proposal**, never the whole list. A pet
food sponsor cares about Amazon Live A-List and Cool Cat Stuff reach, not the
Bigo Live history. Press mentions work best as a single credibility line near
the top — not a bulleted wall.

Never present a credential as "in progress" or "pending" as though it were
confirmed. If it isn't finalised, leave it out.

## The rule that outranks the rest of this file

**Do not fabricate metrics, follower counts, past brand deals, press mentions,
or dollar figures.**

Follower counts, engagement rates, viewer numbers, past sponsors, and reach
figures are deliberately **not** in this file, because they change and because
a confident wrong number in a sponsor's inbox is worse than a blank.

When you need one you don't have, write a bracketed placeholder —
`[INSERT CURRENT FOLLOWER COUNT]` — and leave it for Chris to fill. Never
estimate, never infer from "similar" accounts, never round up from something
you saw once. This is `SAFETY.md` rule five applied to the thing most likely
to tempt you.
