THE ORIGIN STORY

One Hole.
Eight Kittens. One Mission.

In 2023, a pregnant stray cat appeared at a fence hole in our backyard. We took her in. She had seven kittens. We kept them all — and the one stray who showed up later. That decision changed everything.

What started as rescue became a “viral sensation” (Google’s words, not ours). Four Newsweek features in one year. Front page of China Daily. Top 30 Amazon Pet Influencers — two years running. YouTube Partners. The #1 cat channel on Amazon Live.

Now all of it — every view, every dollar, every partnership — funnels into one goal: free veterinary care for cats who need it most.
