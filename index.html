THE ORIGIN

A Fence.
A Cat. A Movement.

In 2023, a pregnant stray cat appeared at a hole in our fence. We took her in. She had seven kittens. We kept them all — plus one more stray who showed up later. That’s eight cats. One family.

The internet took notice. 44 million views on a single post. Four Newsweek features in one year. Front page of China Daily — two years running. Top 30 Amazon Pet Influencers. YouTube Partners. The #1 cat channel on Amazon Live.

But this was never about going viral. Every view, every dollar, every partnership — it all funnels into one thing: free veterinary care for cats who need it most.

The whole family: Frances and all eight cats.
