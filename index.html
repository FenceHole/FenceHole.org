Our Origin Story

How a Hole in a
Fence Built an Empire

Chapter 1

The Hole in the Fence

It all started with an actual hole in a wooden fence. A terrified, feral stray cat named Frances popped her head through that hole looking for food. That single moment of curiosity changed everything.

Chapter 2

Four Months of Trust

Our founder, Chris, spent four months earning Frances's trust—one meal at a time. Then one day she showed up injured, leading Chris to bring her inside. An act of radical patience that would ripple out to hundreds of thousands of people.

Chapter 3

Half a Million Witnesses

Frances gave birth to kittens in Chris's basement while half a million people watched the live stream. A raw, unscripted, deeply human moment that the internet couldn't look away from. Chris kept the entire bonded family.

Chapter 4 — Today

A Viral Sensation Turned Empire

That organic, mental-health-saving journey exploded into a viral sensation that caught the attention of global media. That single act of kindness built the foundation for the Fence Hole LLC media empire—and we're just getting started.
