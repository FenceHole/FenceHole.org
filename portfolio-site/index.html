ORIGIN STORY

How a Hole in a Fence
Built an Empire

The Hole

It started with an actual hole in a wooden fence. A terrified, feral stray cat — Frances — popped her head through that gap looking for food. One small moment. One ordinary fence.

Four Months of Trust

Our founder Chris spent four months earning Frances's trust — one small step at a time. No shortcuts. No forcing it. Just showing up every day until she let him in.

The Turning Point

Then Frances showed up injured. Chris brought her inside. What happened next changed everything: she gave birth to kittens in his basement — while half a million people watched live.

The Family Stays

Chris kept the entire bonded family. What started as an act of compassion became a story of community, mental health, and authentic connection — something the internet desperately needed.

Global Reach

That organic moment exploded into a viral phenomenon — landing in Newsweek four times in one year, on the front page of China Daily, and building a community of hundreds of thousands worldwide. That single act of kindness built the foundation for the Fence Hole LLC media empire.
